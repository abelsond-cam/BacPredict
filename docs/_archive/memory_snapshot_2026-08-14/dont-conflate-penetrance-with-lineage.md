When a per-gene / per-region LR ranking surfaces a gene that isn't a traditional catalogue determinant (e.g. `iME4` for Kp tetracycline at 18% penetrance; `xhlA` for amikacin; the high LR-only bars in the causal plots), describe ONLY what is known: a **high-penetrance gene that is not a traditional catalogue determinant**. Do NOT call it a "lineage correlate" (or assert it tracks sub-lineage structure).

**Why:** whether such a hit is lineage-linked OR genuinely causal is an **open hypothesis, not a conclusion**. David flagged this repeatedly (2026-07-21) as a "mistaken memory" I keep repeating even after it was deleted once. Labelling it lineage prematurely asserts an unproven causal story and biases the whole interpretation.

**How to apply:** neutral language — "high-penetrance non-catalogue gene", "not a traditional/catalogue determinant", "penetrance ~X%". Reserve "lineage-linked" or "causal" for when there is actual evidence for one or the other. Ties to [[amr-ladder-raw-recovery-framing]] (report raw recovered AUROC; don't net out lineage/confounds).
